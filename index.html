Simple by Design. Perfect Every Time.

Two products, one principle. Choose your style below and see exactly how it works.

  1. 01

    Freeze the Chill Cradle™

    Detach the Chill Cradle™ from the stemless base and place it in your freezer for at least 3 hours until frozen solid. Keep a spare ready for back-to-back glasses.

  2. 02

    Attach to the Base

    Once frozen, attach the Chill Cradle™ back onto its base. The magnetic connection seats it in place, steady and ready to hold your glass.

  3. 03

    Rest Your Glass & Pour

    Set your stemless wine glass inside the Chill Cradle™. Pour your wine and enjoy — lift the glass to sip as normal. Nothing enters the glass. Pure, undiluted flavor.

  4. 04

    Wipe & Refreeze

    Detach the Chill Cradle™ from the base, wipe with a dry towel, and return it to the freezer. Ready for your next glass tonight, tomorrow, indefinitely.
